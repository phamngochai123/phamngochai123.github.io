Last updated: 5 November 2026

ENGLISH
======================================================================

This policy explains how Moon Cat ("the app", package com.haipn.pixelhop) handles information.

THE SHORT VERSION
- Moon Cat has no accounts, no servers of its own, and does not collect your name, email or contact details.
- Photos you choose as hero pictures stay on your device. They are never uploaded.
- The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may collect some device information as described below.

INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE
The app saves the following only on your device, in its private storage:
- your best distance and your last selected hero;
- the photos you choose to use as heroes (resized copies) and the names you give your heroes.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. You can also remove any hero photo inside the app (tap the red X on its card).

PHOTOS
When you tap "Upload photo", the app opens Android's system photo picker. You choose the photos; the app does not request access to your whole photo library. The selected photos are resized and stored privately on your device and are used only to draw your hero in the game.

ADVERTISING (GOOGLE ADMOB)
Moon Cat shows ads using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Depending on your region, settings and consent, AdMob and its partners may collect and use:
- your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
- approximate location (derived from your IP address);
- information about how you interact with ads and the app, and diagnostic data (such as crash logs).

This is used to serve and measure ads, to prevent fraud and to improve the services. We do not receive this data about you ourselves. Google's use of data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy

Consent and choices:
- If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads, and you can change your choice later.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

CHILDREN
Moon Cat is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

DATA SHARING
We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives data through the app is Google (AdMob), as described above.

SECURITY
Data sent to ad networks is transmitted over encrypted connections. Data stored on your device is kept in the app's private storage.

CHANGES
We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
